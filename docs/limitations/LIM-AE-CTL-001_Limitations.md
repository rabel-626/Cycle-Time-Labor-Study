# Cycle Time & Labor Study — Limitations

- Timing quality depends on observer technique and process definition.
- Short studies may not capture product, operator, shift, or equipment variation.
- Mathematical capacity does not prove ergonomic or physical feasibility.
- Takt comparisons require a correct demand/reference rate.
- Support-work assumptions can materially affect labor conclusions.
- The tool does not replace safety analysis, ergonomic assessment, work measurement policy, or site-required validation.
